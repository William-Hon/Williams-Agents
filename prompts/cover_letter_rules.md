# Cover Letter Generation Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Targeted Motivation**: Clearly explain why the candidate is enthusiastic about this specific company and role.
2. **Grounded Narrative**: Pick 1 or 2 relevant projects or work experiences from `data/experiences.json` that directly match the role's responsibilities.
3. **Tone**: Professional, confident, humble, and concise. Avoid fluff or clichés ("I have always dreamed of...").
4. **No Fabrication**: Never claim familiarity with tools or frameworks not in the candidate profile.

## Structure
1. **Header**: Name, contact details, date, company name, position title.
2. **Opening Paragraph**: Role being applied for, alignment with team mission, high-level thesis.
3. **Body Paragraph 1**: Most relevant professional or technical experience demonstrating core skills needed for the role.
4. **Body Paragraph 2**: Secondary experience or research project showing problem solving, AI/data engineering, or collaboration.
5. **Closing Paragraph**: Reiterate interest, availability dates, appreciation, call to action.

## Output Target
- Relative path: `generated/<company-role-application_id>/William_Hon_CoverLetter_<Company>.pdf`
